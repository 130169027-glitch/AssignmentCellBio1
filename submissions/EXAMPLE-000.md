One thing I found fascinating was how the endoplasmic reticulum and
Golgi apparatus work together like a factory assembly line to fold,
tag, and ship proteins to their exact destination in the cell. During
the lab session where we looked at rough ER under the electron
micrograph, it was surprising how much internal organization a single
cell has — it's not just a "bag of enzymes" but a coordinated system
with its own internal logistics.
